What HouseBrief is

HouseBrief is a free iPhone and iPad app. You enter a few details about your property — address, condition, your situation, your timeline — and our internal acquisitions team reviews it. If we’re interested, we reach out directly with a cash-offer conversation.

We are a principal buyer. Not a broker, not an agent, not a listing service. There is no MLS listing, no open houses, no commission. Submitting takes about five minutes and there is no obligation.
