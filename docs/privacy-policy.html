HouseBrief — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-04-19.

Short version We collect what you submit about your property, your contact information, and the photos/documents you upload. We use it to review your property, talk to you about a possible cash offer, and keep audit records. We don't sell your data, don't run behavioral ads, and don't use tracking SDKs. The only parties we share data with are our affiliated Acquisition Entity and contracted vendors (title, legal, inspection) when we're actively pursuing a purchase.

1. Who we are

HouseBrief is a submission intake app operated by a private home-buying company (the "Acquisition Entity") and its affiliates. We are a principal buyer. We are not a real estate broker, agent, licensee, intermediary, or fiduciary. We do not represent buyers or sellers.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide:

  • Contact information (name, email, phone number).
  • Property information (address, ZIP, property type, year built, bedrooms/bathrooms, approximate square footage, condition, known repairs, occupancy, approximate timeline, optional asking expectation).
  • Situation information you choose to share (for example: whether the property is inherited, in probate, has tenants, has tax or lien concerns, or has code violation notices).
  • Uploaded photos or documents (e.g. deed, mortgage statement, tax bill, inspection report).
  • Any follow-up answers you provide when our team requests more information.

Information we collect automatically:

  • A per-install user identifier so we can associate submissions with your account.
  • Basic app-usage and crash information used to diagnose bugs. We do not use any third-party analytics SDK in the app. Any diagnostic data is aggregated and server-side.
  • Standard device metadata sent in ordinary HTTP requests (e.g. user-agent, IP address) for security, fraud prevention, and debugging. IP addresses are not linked to any ad-targeting system.

Information we may obtain from public sources: Once you submit a property, we may pull public records (county tax records, GIS data, deed filings, MLS historical data only where permitted, approximate valuation data) to evaluate the submission. These sources are not combined with your PII to build a marketing profile — they are used strictly to evaluate the property.

We do not request or collect: precise device location, microphone input, contacts, calendar, Apple Health, advertising identifiers, or data for behavioral advertising.

3. How we use your information

  • To evaluate your property submission.
  • To follow up with you with questions or a possible direct cash offer.
  • To comply with legal and recordkeeping obligations (including retention of signed purchase agreements).
  • To prevent fraud, enforce our Terms, and secure our systems.
  • To improve the quality of the review process (internal only — we do not share training data with third parties).

4. AI-assisted review

We use Anthropic's Claude model, accessed via API, to help our internal team summarize and score submissions. When we run this analysis, we send the text of your submission to Anthropic's API. Per Anthropic's commercial API terms, that data is not used to train Anthropic's models. We do not send photos or uploaded documents to Claude.

5. Who we share information with

  • The Acquisition Entity — the direct buyer that may pursue your property. This is an affiliate of HouseBrief.
  • Contracted service vendors — title companies, closing attorneys, inspection providers, insurance providers, photo-hosting/infrastructure providers. These vendors act only on our instructions and are contractually prohibited from using your data for their own marketing.
  • Legal and regulatory authorities — where required by law, subpoena, court order, or to protect our legal rights.
  • Successors — in the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may be transferred to the successor entity, subject to the commitments in this Privacy Policy.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not rent or trade your information.

6. Retention

Submission records (including photos and documents) are retained for the longer of: (a) the duration of any active or completed transaction plus any applicable tax, fair-housing, or real-estate recordkeeping period; or (b) seven years, which is standard for financial and real-estate records. If a submission does not result in a contract, we will retain the submission in an audit-only form but will honor deletion or anonymization requests on the schedule described below.

7. Your rights (CCPA / U.S. state privacy laws)

Depending on your state, you may have the right to:

  • request access to the personal information we hold about you;
  • request correction of inaccurate personal information;
  • request deletion or anonymization of your personal information, subject to our recordkeeping obligations;
  • opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do not engage in either, so this is automatically honored);
  • be free from unlawful discrimination for exercising these rights.

To exercise any of these rights, use Settings → Delete account in the app, or email privacy@medbillresolve.com. We respond within 45 days.

8. Notifications

If you enable push notifications, we send generic alerts (for example: "We have a quick follow-up on your property submission"). Notification payloads never contain your property address or any financial amount. You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings.

9. Security

Data in transit is encrypted via TLS. Uploaded photos and documents are stored with at-rest AES-256 encryption. Access to submissions is limited to authorized personnel and is audit-logged. We apply the principle of least privilege across our systems.

10. Children

HouseBrief is not intended for use by anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. International users

HouseBrief is available only to users in the United States and is intended only for properties located in the United States.

12. Data brokers and "Do Not Sell"

We are not a data broker under California or other applicable state law. We do not sell personal information. Even so, if you would like a written confirmation of this fact for your records, email privacy@medbillresolve.com.

13. Changes to this Policy

If we change this Policy in a material way, we will notify you through the app and update the Effective date above. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated Policy.
