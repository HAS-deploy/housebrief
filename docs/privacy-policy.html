HouseBrief — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-04-19. DRAFT — must be reviewed by counsel before launch.

Who we are

HouseBrief is a submission intake app for a private home-buying company. We are not a real estate broker, agent, or representative. The affiliated acquisition entity behind HouseBrief may directly purchase properties it finds a fit after review.

What we collect

  • Contact: name, email, phone.
  • Property details: address, ZIP, property type, condition, occupancy, situation details you choose to share, timeline, photos, optional documents.
  • Device: a per-install user identifier so we can tie your submissions to your account.

How we use it

  • To review your submission internally.
  • To contact you with follow-up questions or a possible cash offer.
  • To maintain an audit trail of submissions and interactions.

Who we share it with

We share submission data only with:

  • Our affiliated acquisition entity (the direct buyer).
  • Contracted vendors (title, legal, inspection) only when we're actively pursuing a purchase.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share with advertisers. We do not use tracking SDKs.

Data retention

Submissions and associated photos/documents are retained indefinitely for audit purposes unless you request deletion. Upon deletion, your contact info is removed; submissions may be anonymized and retained for recordkeeping.

Your rights (CCPA-aligned)

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. Email hello@housebrief.example or use the "Delete account" option in Settings.

Notifications

If you enable push notifications, we send generic alerts ("We have a quick follow-up on your submission"). Push payloads never contain addresses or financial amounts.

Children

HouseBrief is not intended for use by anyone under 18.

Security

Uploaded photos and documents are stored with at-rest encryption. Access is limited to authorized personnel. All admin access is logged.

Changes

We'll update this policy when our practices change. The "last updated" date reflects the latest change.
